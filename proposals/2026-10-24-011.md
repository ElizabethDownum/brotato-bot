## Why
Run 9 (`runs/20261024-111641-well_rounded-d1.md`) died on wave 19. It took 142 damage from 6 hits in about 18 s, with 103 enemies on screen; no earlier wave had more than 53. Survival stats slid through the late waves: armor went from 0 to -3 and HP regen from +1 to -3 between waves 14 and 18, while max HP reached only 78. The trades that caused this were Sunglasses, Gummy Berserker x2, Broken Mouth x2 and Fresh Meat; each was scored as a gain because armor (4) and regen (2) were weighted below the damage stats. This plan raises armor 4 → 7, HP regen 2 → 5 and max HP 5 → 7, so the scorer prices those trades as losses. Everything else is proposal 10: speed 4.0, which held speed at 22 late; arm B steering; and the same `ask_on` review list.

Mod sync 6 also ships with this run (commits bcee839, 24c7eb7, 9785aa1). Haiku now rules approve or decline on every option it is asked about. Review-list items need approval, and an item that is declined is barred until the next wave. In run 9, items Haiku argued against were bought by the rules anyway: Weird Ghost, Gentle Alien x3 and Bait.


## Risk
Two changes ship at once, so a better result cannot be split between the weights and the vetoes. The decision rows (rulings, missing counts) will show what the vetoes did. Raising the survival weights takes score away from damage. If kill speed falls, the crowd can still build up at waves 18-20; compare kills per wave with run 9. The rulings are new: if Haiku leaves options unruled, review-list items default to decline and other items fall back to the rules.
